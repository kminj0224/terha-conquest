# v1.11.69 ground contact and looping smoke

Explosion artwork now aligns its opaque lower edge to the struck unit's actual rendered foot/track/wheel edge. Blood and sparks retain their original impact coordinates. City explosion coordinates retain the previous behavior. Every dirt/fire keyframe has an offline alpha-foot measurement, preventing transparent image margins from lifting the blast off the target's feet.

`impact-ground-data.js` contains offline measurements from the real national sprite renderer, including direction, firing state and four formation levels. `tools/generate-impact-ground.cjs` regenerates it using the native QA harness. Runtime rendering uses numeric lookup only, without reading pixels or creating canvases. Fatal hits retain a bounded recent-target snapshot so an air strike can still anchor its explosion after the victim is removed.

Smoke now begins at the same instant as the burst. Five equally sized, fully formed plume illustrations loop at 65 ms per keyframe (325 ms per cycle) while their opacity decreases over approximately 3000 ms. They animate internal turbulence, rather than playing a growing/dying puff once or fading a single still. Smoke is drawn behind the burst. The burst keeps its v68 timing; smoke overlaps it instead of extending the effect by another three seconds. Wreck smoke uses the same looping artwork.

The latest smoke plume on each target tile replaces older overlapping plumes. At most 24 tile plumes paint simultaneously; this limit never hides initial explosion bursts. Offscreen culling, AI animation skipping, city-only garrison impacts and unit depth ordering are retained. Overlapping smoke and burst costs one additional image draw during the short burst, with no additional timers or sound events.

## Artwork

Built-in image generation, transparent edit referenced from the original wreck smoke. Final asset: `game/assets/smoke-loop.webp`, five 192px square keyframes. Prompt: five equal-size fully formed charcoal-black smoke plumes on a fixed baseline, same height/width/density, realistic internal turbulent curls, short chronological phases with seamless last-to-first looping; no growth or source-image fade; transparent feathered edges and bottom; no fire, ground, text or checkerboard. Runtime controls opacity independently.
