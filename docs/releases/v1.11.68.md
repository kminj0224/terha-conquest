# v1.11.68 combat VFX

Non-artillery explosion bursts last 1.5 times their previous duration. Ground dirt bursts use five chronological illustrations, retaining the previous maximum-size dirt image at frame three. Tank/AT mechanical hits retain an initial large spark. Ordinary rifle and motorized-infantry hits retain blood/sparks rather than explosions.

Artillery, aircraft (including helicopters), naval and strategic explosive impacts use seven fire/smoke frames: the previous four originals and three new intermediate illustrations. Artillery burst timing remains unchanged. All explosive impacts then have a separate 3000 ms, five-frame black-smoke tail with gradual fading. Mechanical wreck smoke uses the same sequence during its existing three-second lifetime. Anti-tank launch smoke deliberately uses the original seven subtle procedural puffs.

Burst feet and smoke drawing rectangles stay fixed. The internal smoke plume rises through its illustrations. Soft transparent feet, city-only garrison impacts, splash victim effects and world depth ordering remain intact. Drawing a tail does not create another sound or effect object. Pre-scheduled helicopter impacts receive the same classification/timing as normal impacts.

## Performance

Shared 192 px WebP strips: `dirt-sequence.webp` (5), `smoke-sequence.webp` (5), `explosion-sequence.webp` (7). Each stage paints one frame. No steady-state Canvas surfaces or pixel reads. Screen culling and AI animation skipping remain. Residual impact smoke merges by target tile, keeps the latest plume and is capped at 24 visible tile plumes; initial impact bursts are unaffected.

Native Canvas component medians on this host (not physical Android FPS): v67/v68 explosion 59/59 microseconds; single smoke 56/54 microseconds; 24 simultaneous smoke plumes 1358/1515 microseconds. The 0.157 ms difference in the extreme smoke sample does not indicate a material regression. The additional decoded strips cost approximately 2 MiB relative to previous effect textures, shared by every effect. Encoded new strips total about 225 KiB.

## Artwork production

Built-in image generation, transparent-background edits of existing dirt-impact, wreck-smoke and explosion-soft references. Production prompts: (1) five same-camera, fixed-baseline realistic dirt/debris blast stages, no fire, previous peak at the middle, two collapsing/settling stages; (2) five charcoal-smoke stages, initial puff through rising/billowing plume to thin dissipating wisps, fixed sprite foot and soft transparency, no fire; (3) three intermediate military fire/smoke frames between the previous four originals. Frame extraction, downsampling, soft-alpha preparation and atlas assembly are performed offline. Existing peak/original fire frames are retained.

## Verification

`qa/combat-sequence-native.cjs` checks distinct 5/5/7 images, source alpha, exact burst/tail timings, fixed smoke rectangle, fade, original AT puff renderer, same-tile merge, visible bound, and no Canvas allocations. The full native suite also reports its existing mission02 autoplay result; do not infer physical-device frame rate from these checks.
