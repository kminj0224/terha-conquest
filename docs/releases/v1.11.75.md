# v1.11.75 — Conquest AI difficulty and supply

- Conquest-only Dante AI starting resources and gold/food/oil income: Easy 1x, Normal 1.5x, Hard 3x. Human Dante receives no AI multiplier. Campaign multipliers remain unchanged.
- Dante AI pays movement and attack oil on Easy/Normal, subject to normal fuel-efficiency and unit exceptions. Hard movement/attack oil is zero, including carriers. Production, aircraft missions and research retain their own resource costs.
- Easy gives no starting or recurring free transports to any AI. Player starting fleets, paid production and saved units/cargo remain intact. Normal/Hard retain existing grants and caps.
- AI turns attempt paid airport transport captures of all affordable, reachable, undefended enemy cities at zero durability immediately after income, before city upgrades, ordinary army orders and discretionary spending. Existing airport/range/radiation/occupancy/war checks apply. Campaign orders are unchanged.
- Specials still have a faction-wide one-order-per-turn limit. Available production sites are ranked by distance to hostile land borders, falling back to enemy cities for overseas fronts. Normal production eligibility still applies, including homeland and level requirements.
- Tianje Lake water components no longer count as ocean coastline or supply-network transit. Actual friendly land and open Hansa bridges still connect routes. Invalid deliveries pause through the existing queue rules rather than deleting units.
- Actual-map lake-only coastal regions audited: 서한사, 해룡항, 금해. Regression tests exercise isolated occupation production for both human and AI, plus lake-crossing rejection.
